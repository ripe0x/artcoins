| D71 | independent audit ACV2-01 (high): grants and budget must stay backed by the transaction's net canonical flow. the token keeps, per transaction and direction, the cumulative canonical flow the hook attested and the cumulative amount consumed from it. an attestation in the opposite direction first cancels unused allowance; if the remaining net canonical flow in a direction falls below what was already consumed in that direction, the call reverts and the whole unlock reverts.
